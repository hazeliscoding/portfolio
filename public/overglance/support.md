# Support

Overglance is made by one person, so support is direct and usually quick, but not instant.

## Bugs and ideas

Open an issue at [overglance-releases](https://github.com/hazeliscoding/overglance-releases/issues). Attach a bug report: **Copy bug report** in the tray puts one on the clipboard and keeps it at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Overglance\bug-report.txt`. It holds your version and Windows version, the settings that shape what shows, your calendars by name, which agents have hooks, whether your copy is a trial or licensed, and each network use with the hosts it reached and when. It never holds a link, a key, an address, a path or a location; a calendar named by an email address and a server of your own show as such, not by name.

## Purchases, keys and refunds

Your receipt email from Polar links to your customer portal, where your key, your activations and refunds live. Anything the portal can't do, send to the support contact below with the email address you bought with.

## Security

Report a vulnerability privately with **Report a vulnerability** on the [Security tab of overglance-releases](https://github.com/hazeliscoding/overglance-releases/security), not in a public issue. Reports especially wanted: Overglance reading a credential file or a token, any network call beyond the eleven its README lists, a hook or status line that slows or breaks an agent, a calendar link or key reaching a log, a message or a report, and the pill taking focus or showing over a fullscreen app. Fixes go into the next release, and releases are code-signed with SHA-256 checksums in `SHA256SUMS`.
