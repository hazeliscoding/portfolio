# Refunds

**A full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked.** There's a 7-day trial before you buy, so you'll know what you're getting; if it still isn't for you, say so within 14 days of the purchase and the money comes back.

## How

Ask through Polar's customer portal, the link in your receipt email, or the [support contact](SUPPORT.md) with the email address you bought with. Polar, the merchant of record, makes the refund to the card you paid with; banks take a few days to show it.

## What happens to the key

A refunded key stops working at its next weekly check, within a week on each PC it was on, and the panel goes back to showing where to buy. Your settings and data stay on your PC.

## After 14 days

Refunds past 14 days are at Hazel Granados's discretion. A bug that stops Overglance doing what its README says counts; a change of mind, after 14 days and a 7-day trial, usually doesn't. Where the law where you live gives you more, you have more.
